Shipping Policy

Our estimated delivery time is 3–5 business days after order confirmation. Delivery may vary depending on location, courier availability, and unforeseen delays. Contact us for assistance with delayed orders.

Return and Refund Policy

Contact KitBench within 7 days of delivery to request a return. Include your order details and the reason for the request. Return eligibility will be reviewed and confirmed by KitBench.

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us promptly with your order details and supporting photographs where available.

Before a return is completed, we will explain the return procedure and any applicable return-shipping charges. Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method, subject to payment-provider processing times.
